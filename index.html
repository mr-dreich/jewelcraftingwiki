JewelcraftingValheim mod · v2.0.11
    Mod reference · built from the v2.0.11 DLL

    Cut gems. Socket gear. Bend the rules.

    Jewelcrafting adds eight colours of gemstone to Valheim. You mine raw formations, cut them into Simple, Advanced and Perfect gems, and socket them into weapons, armour, tools and jewelry. Each gem grants a different effect depending on the slot it sits in.

    All numbers on this page are the mod's default values. Servers can change almost everything through the config file and the YAML files.

    1 · findRaw formationsBreak glowing crystal formations or loot creatures for uncut gemstones.
    2 · cutGemcutters TableCut uncut stones into Simple, then Advanced, then Perfect gems. Each cut can shatter.
    3 · socketAdd socketsAdd up to 3 sockets to any equipment. Failure destroys the item by default.
    4 · combineFuse & synergiseFuse two gems into one, chase synergies, hunt boss gems and world bosses.
    Materials

    Gemstones

    Every colour exists as a raw formation, an uncut gemstone, a shard, and three cut tiers. Higher tiers give stronger effects.

    Where formations spawn

    Gemstone formations appear in groups of 2–6 across every biome. Each biome strongly favours one colour (43% weight) and spawns the others rarely (1%). Formations have 20 HP, drop one uncut gemstone, and respawn after 100 in-game days. 3% are giant formations that are scaled up and drop 2–10 gems.

    Special gems

    Crafting

    Cutting gems

    Gems are cut at the Gemcutters Table. Every attempt can fail. A failed cut destroys the gem and gives you a shard of the same colour.

    Skill bonus is multiplicative by default: chance × (1 + skill/100 × 15%). The Careful Cutting synergy adds +2% on top. Each attempt raises your Jewelcrafting skill.

    Recipes

    • Simple: 1 uncut gemstone, or 5 at once (each rolls separately).
    • Advanced: 1 Simple gem, or 5 at once.
    • Perfect: 1 Advanced gem. Needs a level 2 Gemcutters Table.
    • Bad-luck recipes never fail: 12 shards make a Simple gem, 35 shards make an Advanced gem.

    Astral Gemcutter

    A smelter-style station. Load uncut gemstones or Simple gems and it cuts them over time. It uses the Jewelcrafting skill of the player who loaded it. A failed cut comes out as a shard.

    Vanilla Ashlands gems

    At a level 4 Gemcutters Table you can craft the vanilla Bloodstone-style gems: 2 Perfect Emerald / Ruby / Sapphire + 10 Eitr make 1 green / red / blue vanilla gemstone.

    The core of the mod

    Gem effects

    A gem's effect depends on the slot it is socketed into. Find a colour and slot below, or browse the full list.

    A specific weapon slot overrides the generic one. A red gem gives Fire Starter in most weapons, but Opportunity in an axe. Orange gems only work in knives (Perforation), polearms (Thunderclap) and magic staves. Effects of the same name stack across all your equipment; the stacking rule on each card says how.

    Unique gems

    Boss gems

    Each main boss drops one unique gem. It works in any slot and fires on a timer.

    • By default (Truly Unique) the first kill of each boss in a world drops one gem. Servers can switch to a drop chance (30%, one per player) instead.
    • Only one unique gem can be equipped across all your gear at a time.
    • Each power announces itself with a 4-second Impending… status before activating, then repeats after a random cooldown between the listed minimum and maximum.
    • Boss powers do not fire while you hold a build tool. Servers can also block them inside player bases.
    • Boss gems cannot be fused with coloured gems. The Time Warp synergy shortens their cooldowns.
    Gem combinations

    Synergies

    Synergies count every gem you have equipped, including your weapon. Merged gems count both of their colours. Active synergies show in your inventory.

    Equipment

    Sockets

    Add sockets to equipment at the Gemcutters Table's socketing tab. Every item can hold up to 3 sockets by default.

    Success chances

    Uses the skill slider from Cutting gems. Sockets 4–10 only matter if the server raises the maximum.

    What happens on failure

    When an item breaks you recover 50% of its base crafting cost and 100% of its upgrade costs. An Orb of Misfortune blessing saves the item once. The Sturdy Spinel Ring comes with every socket already added.

    Socket costs (when the server uses item costs)

    Costs depend on the biome the item comes from. Each later socket costs more.

    Socketing rules

    • Open an item's sockets by hovering it in your inventory and pressing the interact key. You can also use the table.
    • You cannot put the same gem (same colour and tier, including merged gems that contain it) twice into one item. Different tiers of the same colour are fine.
    • Gems can be removed again for free by default. The server can add break chances or forbid removal.
    • Hold Left Alt while hovering a socketed item to see exact effect values.
    • With the Azu Extended Player Inventory mod, rings and necklaces get their own slots. Otherwise they use the utility slot. When you wear several utility-type items, their sockets are split between them.
    • Item names and backgrounds are coloured by how many sockets the item has.

    Merged gems

    Two gems of the same tier but different colours can be fused into one merged gem, such as a Perfect Ruby-Sapphire. A merged gem gives both colours' effects in a single socket. See Fusion boxes.

    Merging

    Fusion boxes

    Put two gems of the same tier into a fusion box and press Seal. The box fills up as you play. At 100% the fusion succeeds or both gems shatter into shards.

    How progress fills

    • Activity: once per minute, if you moved (more than 30 m when sailing), a random sealed box in your inventory gains its activity reward.
    • Boss kills: killing a boss within 50 m adds a lump of progress to a random sealed box.
    • Two identical Common or Blessed boxes can be fused in a third box of the same tier to make the next tier up (100%). If that fails you get two random shards.
    • Boxes drop from creatures. Chance = 1 / drop rate × (creature max HP / average HP)⅓, so tougher creatures drop more.

    Boss kill progress

    Gear

    Jewelry

    Rings and necklaces are crafted from a Perfect gem and a vanilla Chain. Necklaces need a level 3 table, rings level 2. Most can be upgraded to quality 4 for 500 Coins per level and gain +1 armor per level.

    Luck (Necklace of Onyx Luck, Sulfur Ring of Luck)

    • Each kill while wearing it adds one stack. Unequipping resets the stacks.
    • Every stack raises the chance of each creature drop by +2%. Drops over 100% roll extra items.
    • Stacks cap at 25 + your Jewelcrafting level (up to 125 stacks, +250% drop chance).
    • Each stack lowers your damage by 1% (to at most −80%) and raises damage taken by 3% (to at most +300%).
    • Stacks above the cap hurt you every 5 seconds. Necklace and ring keep separate stacks, and their luck adds up.
    Modifiers

    Orbs, frames & mirrors

    Drag one of these onto an item (or a gem) in your inventory to use it.

    With default settings, only the Orb of Misfortune drops. The Orbs of Divinity, Whimsicality, Finality, Corruption and Prophecy reroll or randomise gem power ranges. The default gem config gives every gem a fixed value per tier (a Perfect Ruby's Fire Starter is always 15%), so there is nothing to reroll and these five orbs never drop. Frames of Chance and Chaos also have a 0% creature drop rate by default and only come from Mystical Gemstone prizes.

    How to make them drop. Both steps are server-side; with Lock Configuration on, only an admin can change them.

    Step 1: turn on power ranges (pick one option):

    • Option A, quickest: in BepInEx/config/org.bepinex.plugins.jewelcrafting.cfg, section [2 - Socket System], set Effect Power Deviation to a value from 1 to 75 (for example 20). Every non-boss gem then rolls within ±20% of its normal value. That alone makes the five orbs start dropping.
    • Option B, more control: give at least one gem effect a value range in the gem effects YAML (admins can edit it in-game with the Edit Socket config YAML button in the config manager, or in a Jewelcrafting.*.yml file in BepInEx/config). Replace a fixed list with ranges, for example:
      fire starter:
        slot: [ weapon, elementalmagic ]
        gem: red
        power: [ 3-7, 8-12, 13-17 ]

    With either option, Random Power Ranges (default On) makes each effect on a gem roll separately.

    The Corrupted Gem's own −10 to +10 range does not count. The orb descriptions below are for servers that enable ranges.

    What corruption does

    An Orb of Corruption picks one of four outcomes with equal chance, then locks the item. Corrupted items and gems can no longer be changed.

    OutcomeResult
    Quality shiftItem quality goes up or down by one level (minimum 1).
    Socket shiftEither a random socket is removed, or a socket with a random corrupted gem is added.
    Full rerollQuality becomes random (can exceed the normal maximum by one) and the item gets 1–4 sockets filled with random corrupted gems.
    Corrupted GemA Corrupted Gem is added in a new socket. Its effect rolls between −10 and +10 (see Berserk, Defender, Sprinter, Pain Tolerance, Raging).

    An Orb of Prophecy fixes the outcome in advance: you can preview the corrupted result by holding the advanced-tooltip key, then use an Orb of Corruption to make it real.

    Exploration

    Finding gems

    Creature drops
    Every kill has a 1.5% chance per colour to drop that uncut gemstone (8 colours, so about 11% for any gem).
    Crystal Caves
    Up to 150 caves spawn 1–10 km from the world centre in most biomes. Their Crystal Golems (600 HP) drop 4–6 of every uncut gemstone colour, making caves the richest gem source in the game.
    Formations
    See Gemstones. The Humite Necklace of Attunement pins nearby formations on your map.

    Loot systems

    Servers pick one or more loot systems. Gem Drops is the default.

    SystemWhat drops
    Gem DropsUncut gemstones from creatures (default).
    Equipment DropsSocketed equipment with gems already in it. By default you can only get items whose recipe you know. 20% drop chance, 7% from low-HP creatures.
    Gem ChestsA chest with 2–3 gems; take 1 and the chest disintegrates. Blessed and Celestial chests have better tiers.
    Equipment ChestsA chest with 2–3 equipment pieces; take 1.
    Endgame

    World bosses & the Mystical Gemstone

    Every 2 hours of world time a Crystal Reaper appears, frozen in a crystal cage somewhere 1–10 km from the world centre. A timer on the map shows the next spawn.

    The Crystal Reapers

    • Break the cage to release the boss. It despawns 60 minutes later.
    • 10% of spawns are the Frozen Triumvirate event: all three reapers at once.
    • Their explosions apply a vulnerability 5 seconds after the hit: fire, frost or poison damage taken is doubled.
    • Each kill gives every player within 100 m 5 Celestial Coins and adds fusion box progress.
    • Celestial weapons deal +10% damage to them; celestial shields block 10% more.

    The Mystical Gemstone

    The Mystical Gemstone stands in the Meadows, 1–3 km from the world centre; its map icon appears once you have explored it. Offer up to 15 Celestial Coins at the gemstone, then open the chests around it. Each coin is one roll:

    • First the current event's main prizes are rolled (2% each for most celestial gear).
    • Otherwise you get a consolation gem: 2% Perfect, 3% Advanced, 15% Simple, 80% a random shard.
    • Events rotate by real date, one per day, in a 10-day cycle.

    Event prizes

    Celestial gear

    Celestial items cannot be crafted, only won. Upgrade them at a Forge for 5 Celestial Coins per level. Stats below use the default Ashlands balance; servers can choose Plains or Mistlands presets instead.

    In the files, not in the game

    Unused items

    These ship inside the v2.0.11 DLL but nothing in the code makes them obtainable or gives them an effect. You will not find them in normal play.

    The Stealth Archer gem effect is in the same state: it is written in the default gem config but switched off (see Stealth Archer).

    For server admins

    Configuration

    Settings live in org.bepinex.plugins.jewelcrafting.cfg. Gem effects, synergies, socket costs, loot and gacha prizes live in the Jewelcrafting.*.yml files and can be edited in-game by admins.

    Settings needed for orb drops. On default settings the Orbs of Divinity, Whimsicality, Finality, Corruption and Prophecy never drop, and neither do the two frames. To turn them on:
    • Set [2 - Socket System] Effect Power Deviation to 1–75 (or give gems value ranges in the gem effects YAML). This enables the five orbs.
    • Set [7 - Other] Chance Frame Drop Chance and Chaos Frame Drop Chance above 0 to make the frames drop.
    • Tune the other … Orb … Drop Chance settings in [7 - Other] if you want them more or less common.
